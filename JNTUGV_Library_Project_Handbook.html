JNTU-GV CENTRAL LIBRARY
THE TRADITIONAL PARADIGM

THE WAY LIBRARIES WORKED
BEFORE EVERYTHING WAS CONNECTED

Every day, our campus moves through hundreds of academic, research, and circulation activities. But for decades, these operations were scattered across physical accession ledgers, manual slips, isolated spreadsheets, and disconnected desks.

THE WORK DIDN'T LIVE IN ONE PLACE

Paper Ledgers → Manual Slips → Separate Excel Files → Physical Shelf Searching → Paper Fine Receipts

🔍

Catalog Invisibility

Students had no remote access to verify if a textbook was available. Physical visits only to find copies were already issued.

📍

Physical Location Chaos

"Where is this book?" Searching through dozens of racks and shelves manually. Misplaced books became permanently lost.

⏳

Circulation Bottlenecks

Manual handwriting of roll numbers and book IDs. 20-minute waiting queues during exam periods and semester start.

💰

Fines & Due Disputes

Zero automated notifications. Students discovered overdue fines months later during clearance examinations.

🪑

Study Space Scramble

Reading room capacity unknown. Students walking across campus only to find zero available study desks or collaboration zones.

📊

Zero Activity Audits

University management lacked department-wise reading metrics, accreditation stats, and verified audit trails.

!

The Information Existed. The Connection Didn't.

Over 50,000 valuable books sat in the library, but without spatial mapping, instant search, and barcode automation, knowledge remained locked behind paper friction and delays.
